Upgrade your system with the MSI SPATIUM M450 PCIe 4.0 NVMe SSD — built for speed, reliability, and performance. Whether you're gaming, creating, or multitasking, this high-performance storage solution ensures lightning-fast responsiveness and smooth everyday computing.

Powered by advanced 3D NAND flash and designed in a sleek M.2 2280 form factor, the SPATIUM M450 offers blazing sequential read speeds up to 3600 MB/s and write speeds up to 3000 MB/s.
